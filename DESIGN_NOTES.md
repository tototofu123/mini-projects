# Design Notes

## Decisions

- **Color Palette:** Wood colors (Burlywood `#DEB887` and SaddleBrown `#8B4513`) were chosen for the board to represent the physical game's common wooden material. The pawns use vibrant primary colors (Vivid Blue, Vivid Red) to maximize contrast and readability against the wood tones.
- **No Gradients:** Specifically requested by the user. The UI achieves depth through layout and stark contrast, avoiding visual noise.
- **Walls:** Used a solid dark slate color (`#1F2937`) instead of a wood color so they clearly stand out as blockers against both the board surface and the grid squares.
