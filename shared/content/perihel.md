# Perihel

## One-line story

A local-first desktop companion that captures fleeting intentions and resurfaces them when they become useful again.

## What it demonstrates

Perihel is the clearest proof of Toto’s personal product judgement. It is not built for a portfolio prompt; it comes from a real workflow problem: keeping ideas and commitments from disappearing.

## Evidence to add before public launch

Use sanitised captures only. Show the capture-to-review loop, the local-first data decision, and one example of reminder logic without exposing private content.

## Honest limitation

Personal records, local paths, and private ideas must never appear in the public portfolio.
